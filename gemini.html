Founder & Creative Director

A colorist's eye, a sculptor's hand.

“Your bone structure, features, and coloring combined with your vision are all factors we consider to create a personalized look made exclusively for you.”

With over two decades at the forefront of Bergen County's luxury hair care community, Ayfer Deniz is known for intuitive consultation, architectural precision, and multi-dimensional color. As former founder and owner of the renowned Ayfer Salon on Closter Dock Road in Closter, NJ, Ayfer built a loyal clientele who value Manhattan-caliber artistry in a peaceful, welcoming setting.

A lifelong student of fashion, fine art, and contemporary technique, Ayfer has styled hair for runway productions, fashion editorial shoots, and was recognized nationally in People Magazine for her dimensional blondes and rich brunettes.

Today at Artora Beauty Bar on South Summit Street in Tenafly, she welcomes guests to an unhurried, one-on-one studio where European formulas and personalized care take center stage.
