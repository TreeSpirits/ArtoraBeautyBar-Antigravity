Meet Ayfer Deniz

“At Artora, bringing out the very best in you is our mission. Your bone structure, features, and coloring combined with your vision are all factors we consider to create a personalized look made exclusively for you.”

With more than two decades at the forefront of Bergen County’s luxury hair care scene, Ayfer Deniz is celebrated for her intuitive eye, architectural cutting technique, and transformative color work. As the former founder and owner of the beloved Ayfer Salon on Closter Dock Road in Closter, NJ, Ayfer built a devoted following among discerning clients seeking Manhattan-caliber artistry in a warm, welcoming sanctuary.

An avid student of fashion, fine art, and pop culture, Ayfer has styled hair for numerous models, editorial shoots, and runway shows—earning national recognition in People Magazine for her signature multi-dimensional blondes, warm golds, rich brunettes, and vibrant reds.

Today, at Artora Beauty Bar on South Summit Street in Tenafly, Ayfer brings her lifelong passion into an airy, second-floor studio designed for unhurried, one-on-one luxury—personally testing and curating every European and botanical formula that touches your hair.
